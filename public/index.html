One plan, four parts

What you get

  • Budget

    Split by category and adding up to exactly what you can spend, with a contingency reserve and a money-saving tip for every line.

    From a real plan: “Opt for interactive live chaat and street food counters from local Pune caterers instead of a heavy sit-down buffet.”

  • Timeline

    Real dates counted back from your event day. Anything already overdue moves to today and is flagged “Do now”.

    From a real plan: a wedding 17 weeks away moved “Book venue” (normally 44 weeks out) to today.

  • Vendor checklist

    What to look for, questions to ask before booking and a book-by date for each vendor. Tick them off as you go.

    From a real plan: “Is on-site setup and technical troubleshooting included on the day?”

  • Personalised ideas

    Theme, decor, food, activities and guest experience, each explaining why it fits your event.

    From a real plan: “It strictly honors the vegetarian priority while blending playful arcade nostalgia into the menu.”

Three steps

How it works

  1. Describe your event

    Date, city, guest count, budget and currency, plus the vibe and anything that matters: dietary needs, traditions, accessibility.

  2. Gemini drafts your plan

    Gemini chooses the categories, tasks, vendors and ideas. Event Planner then turns them into amounts that add up exactly and real calendar dates.

  3. Refine in plain words

    Ask for “make it cheaper” or “more eco-friendly” and the plan is revised, not restarted.
